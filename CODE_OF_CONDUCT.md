# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, considerate and constructive in project issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code, respect privacy and acknowledge mistakes. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**, with links and relevant context. The maintainer reviews reports confidentially as far as practical and may warn, remove content, restrict participation or exclude participants in proportion to the conduct. Retaliation for good-faith reports is prohibited. Ask for reconsideration privately through the same channel with additional evidence. There is currently no independent moderator or escalation reviewer; independent arbitration cannot be promised for a complaint involving the maintainer.
