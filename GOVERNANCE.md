# Governance

[4alvit](https://github.com/4alvit) is the primary maintainer, responsible for scope, review and merge decisions, repository and CI access, dependency updates, release acceptance, vulnerability response and conduct enforcement. Contributors propose changes through issues and pull requests and supply tests and documentation. Automated checks and reviews provide evidence; the maintainer remains accountable for decisions.

Discuss major changes with their use case, alternatives, compatibility impact and validation. The maintainer resolves disagreements using that evidence and the project's conservative interpretation of sensing observations. Contributors may request reconsideration with new evidence. Private vulnerability and conduct details stay in their documented private channels. New public achievements, including an OpenSSF badge, must be linked accurately from README within 48 hours.

## Continuity limitation

No backup maintainer or established process can restore repository, CI and release access within a week if the primary maintainer becomes unavailable. Bots and publicly forkable source do not establish this capability. OpenSSF Silver access continuity and the two-person bus-factor recommendation remain unmet. A future recovery arrangement requires an authorized person and an actual exercise; none is claimed today.
